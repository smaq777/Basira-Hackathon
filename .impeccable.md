## Design Context

### Users

Arabic Islamic-content writers and editors compare their draft with attributable
sources before publication. The current scope is a single draft up to 3,000 UTF-16
code units, with provisional findings rather than publication or scholarly approval.

### Brand and direction

The owner explicitly approved the current result interface on 5 October 2026,
especially its handling of longer writing. Preserve its Arabic RTL layout, Cairo
typography and quiet editorial presentation. Improve readability within that design.
The owner requested green reference-text cards and light-red draft-difference cards.

### Design principles

- Keep the comparison and actionable differences prominent; collapse extra context.
- Use the same text-type colors and labelled legend on the editor and results.
- Distinguish quotation fidelity, classification and semantic assessment status.
- Bound long editor and loading content; keep scrolling and actions accessible.
- Preserve original Arabic and Unicode text; render all user text as inert content.
- Do not communicate correctness through color alone or imply scholarly approval.
