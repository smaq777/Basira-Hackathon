## Rights, provenance, and evaluation separation

The organizer README limits availability to research purposes and explicitly distinguishes authentic from hallucinated examples. This is not evidence of permission for unrestricted production redistribution. Preserve the pinned README, download hashes, source URLs, and research-only status; resolve publication rights before expanding production access. [Pinned organizer README](https://github.com/Watheq9/IslamicEval2026/blob/8ca8abb8a0f0d96a5ab07d48108e35b7e02d236e/README.md).

The hadith records lack structured edition/publisher, volume/page, canonical number, grade, and grader. Collection membership does not supply an authenticity judgment. Repeated Matn strings and missing Matn require explicit handling; do not collapse distinct full narrations or silently fill metadata. Existing importer truthfully retains nulls and research provenance.

All 317 official development question strings occur in training; answers are separate. Accordingly, official development is response-held-out, not question-held-out. The local classifier derivative groups questions and purges overlapping citation families and normalized snippets; retain its frozen manifests rather than recombining official train/dev at random. Do not use test examples to tune thresholds or claim a test score without gold.

The organizer separates span detection, text/attribution/isnad correctness, correction, and citation relevance. A role label includes claimed quotations even when inaccurate. Task4 relevance can include indirect support and necessary portions of an answer; it is not a whole-author-claim entailment label. Negation, conditions, exclusivity, missing qualifiers, and conflicting interpretation need their own human-adjudicated support cases. [Dataset description](https://sites.google.com/view/islamiceval2026/dataset-description), [Task1](https://sites.google.com/view/islamiceval2026/subtask-1), [Task4](https://sites.google.com/view/islamiceval2026/subtask-4).

Use the pinned official scorer rather than a generic metric: Task4 collapses labels 1/2 to relevant, computes positive-class F1 per question, handles questions with no positives specially, then averages question scores. The phrase macro-averaged F1 does not mean class-macro F1 here. Corrections and literal `خطأ` must retain their task semantics; missing attribution/isnad validity is not an invented negative label. [Pinned Task4 scorer](https://github.com/Watheq9/IslamicEval2026/blob/8ca8abb8a0f0d96a5ab07d48108e35b7e02d236e/Scoring_scripts/task4_scoring.py).

The competition pages impose a 13B model limit. Current provider models have not been shown to meet that limit; do not describe the current product as an eligible IslamicEval competition submission. This does not invalidate research use or hackathon demonstrations. [Official task overview](https://sites.google.com/view/islamiceval2026/home).

## Small offline check of the current implementation

Frozen protocol `OFFLINE_ROLE_PROTOCOL.json` selects six snippets per role by deterministic hash from the existing family-purged `heldout.small.jsonl`, retaining official origin and question group. Current repository `pipeline.evidence.EvidenceStore.route` ran against the actual local index, with no model calls. On 30 snippets it proposed a mapped role for 9, agreed with the gold role for 3, and abstained on 21. Six Matn examples all abstained; two isnad and four derived-other examples were routed as hadith candidates. These hints are not authentication decisions.

This bounded check exposes conservative routing coverage and the difference between the live rule router and the earlier trained classifier. It supplies snippet boundaries, includes derived-other labels, and reuses an already evaluated holdout; it is not a new blind test, official Task1 span score, or full runtime quality estimate. Exact selection, mapping, per-example IDs, result, and protocol hash are external in `OFFLINE_ROLE_RESULT.json`. Broader evaluation should freeze untouched question/family groups before tuning.

## Actual records flow and documentation gaps

1. Immutable document revision/hash enters local Python intake. It discovers quotations/references against the large local source index and returns original snapshots plus revision/corpus bindings. Its rule routes are discovery hints, not a trained rhetorical classifier.
2. Optional semantic extraction proposes author claims. Bounded hosted hybrid retrieval searches the separate 62-passage research corpus, expands linked context, and passes exact original evidence into the semantic assessor. Local intake corpus fingerprint and hosted retrieval corpus version are separate traced identities.
3. Review completion stores the revision-bound report JSON, durable evidence originals/hashes/attribution, context, footnotes, relations, and quotation findings through the lease-bound report store. Semantic claims/assessments and retrieval/provider trace are currently persisted in report JSON; they are not normalized into the proposed `claim`/`finding_evidence` domain model. Evidence from a separate hosted corpus database cannot assume a same-database `passage_id` join.
4. Pending source approval and provisional author-claim results remain research boundaries. A successful quote match or Task4 relevance label does not approve the claim or publication. No dataset-derived role classifier is silently enabled.

`docs/architecture/SYSTEM_BLUEPRINT.md` and `DATA_MODEL.md` describe an earlier baseline and planned relationships; their runtime/status descriptions need reconciliation with current server, worker, JSON persistence, and separate corpus/report databases. The normalized confirmed-claim workflow remains a future model, not evidence that confirmation already occurs. Current authoritative implementation is `review-worker.ts`, `review-store.ts`, `claim-retrieval.ts`, `semantic-assessment.ts`, `hosted-corpus.ts`, and migrations 0006–0009. The local-versus-hosted corpus coverage gap is material for sermons beyond the small hosted topics.

## Scoped implementation proposal

1. Add a typed dataset registry and offline benchmark importer first: pin revision/hash/rights, keep benchmark responses and gold exclusively in evaluation storage, preserve offsets and conditional N/A labels, and export official scorer-compatible predictions. Run oracle-span and end-to-end tasks separately; report sample count, coverage, precision/recall, abstentions, latency, and corpus version.
2. Evaluate the saved character classifier in shadow mode on untouched mixed-document question/family groups before any routing change. Its output may propose a source role; retrieval/fidelity checks still establish source matches. Measure whether it improves Matn/isnad discovery without adding false evidence.
3. Prepare a research-only source manifest for a small topic-diverse batch of clean reference originals. Preserve full narration and matching Matn as distinct fields, resolve duplicate families and missing-Matn behavior, and retain unknown numbering/grade/edition. Require provenance and rights decisions before production approval. Do not bulk-embed all 34,994 records merely because they are downloaded.
4. Add a separate human-labeled sermon support pack for conditions, negation, superlatives, competing interpretations, and citation relevance. Compare initial quotations, claim-driven retrieval, and complete-context assessment on frozen evidence; preserve failures and unsupported outcomes. Benchmark gold never enters production prompts or retrieval.
5. Reconcile architecture/data-model documentation and expose honest run/source lineage. Normalize semantic claim records later only if the product needs editable confirmations, cross-run analysis, or transactional claim/evidence relationships; current durable JSON is already reviewable.

For judging, the defensible strengths are original preservation, traceable source lineage, explicit research approval, task-specific evaluation, and demonstrated failure handling. Existing classifier metrics are useful experiment evidence, not a claim of broad Islamic correctness or official benchmark rank. The dataset helps measure and expand quotation handling; it alone cannot solve missing scholarly context or prove reasoning support.
