# Specific review reasons and capability-bound ticket action

Related to [#113](https://github.com/smaq777/Basira-Hackathon/issues/113).
The retained mixed-writing UI reports displayed a generic insufficient-source
heading and promised specialist review even when their author claim had a
completed provisional supported finding. Pending source approval and faithful
excerpt extent do not establish missing evidence or an unavailable service.

## Change

The report now presents a neutral `مواضع تحتاج مراجعة` heading with deduplicated
reasons from existing typed findings: unresolved/different quotation wording,
partial source transport, bounded retrieval limitations, unsupported/contradicted
claim determinations, unreviewed author coverage, incomplete assessment, proposed
inference mapping and source approval state. Pending, rejected and revoked sources
have distinct wording. An explicitly approved research-only source receives a
separate research-context limitation, without claiming its approval is pending. A fully explained faithful excerpt with no applicable
inference does not become incomplete solely from overall partial status. Legacy
partial reports lacking a specific explanation retain an honest general fallback.

Support determinations, literal comparisons, original text, source identities and
report hashes are unchanged. A supported provisional finding can coexist with
its pending-approval limitation. Provider language and historical findings are
rendered unchanged as inert content; this presentation fix does not repair their
wording or the historical locator finding.

The selected API's existing public capability is loaded with caller cancellation
and per-review stale-response protection. Only literal `reviewTickets: true`
allows the ticket action; pending, false, absent, malformed, failed and late
responses cannot enable it. A navigation change cancels the old lookup. The
report remains readable and no lookup automatically submits, creates a session
or repeats analysis. Server authorization/readiness remains authoritative. The
copy does not promise a specialist service.

## Validation and boundary

Offline regressions cover complete approved reports; faithful exact/orthographic
excerpts; supported findings with pending approval; unresolved/different wording;
actual context/retrieval/assessment failures; missing evidence; unselected author
coverage; not-applicable writing; proposed mapping; rejected/revoked coexistence;
and unknown legacy partial state. Strict capability true/false/missing/malformed,
network failure, caller abort and late navigation are tested without providers.
Input/report byte preservation is asserted in presentation controls.

The lead owns a separately frozen read-only saved-report browser preview. No
paid model, source acquisition, ticket submission, environment file or Neon
mutation is part of this implementation. Reverting the UI change affects future
presentation only; retained reports and their source versions remain unchanged.
Owner acceptance and deployment remain separate.
