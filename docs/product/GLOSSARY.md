# Islamic terminology and review vocabulary

This is a product terminology guide, not a religious ruling. Arabic wording protects distinctions that may be lost in translation.

| English term                 | Arabic                  | Meaning in Basirah                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quranic verse                | آية قرآنية              | A verse identified by surah and verse number; original text is preserved               |
| Quranic exegesis             | التفسير                 | An attributed explanation of a verse, tied to a named work and edition                 |
| Hadith                       | حديث                    | A report whose wording, source and attributed grading must be distinguished            |
| Chain of transmission        | السند / الإسناد         | The chain of narrators; not the same as an AI-generated evidence trail                 |
| Hadith text                  | متن الحديث              | The report's wording, separate from its transmission chain                             |
| Source tracing               | التخريج                 | Locating a report in its sources; not an automatic independent authenticity verdict    |
| Attributed scholarly grading | حكم المحدّث             | A named scholar's assessment as reported by a source; the model must not invent one    |
| Personal fatwa               | فتوى شخصية              | A religious ruling applied to someone's circumstances; outside the MVP                 |
| Scholarly disagreement       | الخلاف العلمي           | Attributed differing interpretations; not a majority vote among models                 |
| Quotation fidelity           | دقة الاقتباس            | Whether the submitted quotation matches the retrieved source wording                   |
| Attribution accuracy         | صحة النسبة              | Whether the named author, work, verse or reference matches the evidence                |
| Claim support                | مدى دعم الدليل للادعاء  | Whether the cited material supports this specific conclusion within the reviewed scope |
| Unsupported generalization   | التعميم غير المدعوم     | Extending a bounded statement to cases not supported by the cited material             |
| Omitted qualification        | حذف قيد مؤثر            | Removing a condition or qualification that changes what the evidence supports          |
| Unsupported exclusivity      | ادعاء الحصر غير المدعوم | Claiming “only” or excluding alternatives without support in the evidence              |
| Insufficient evidence        | الأدلة غير كافية        | The available approved evidence cannot justify a determination; not proof of falsity   |

## Critical distinction

Basirah's **evidence trail** is a technical record linking a submitted claim to source passages and checks. It is not a scholarly reconstruction of **السند**. Do not market the system as independently authenticating every hadith or adjudicating every Islamic interpretation.

Example wording for an inconclusive result:

> لم نجد ضمن المصادر المتاحة ما يكفي لدعم هذا الاستنتاج. هذا لا يعني أن العبارة باطلة؛ تحتاج إلى مراجعة إضافية.

The English interpretation is: the reviewed sources were insufficient to support the conclusion. This is a statement about the evidence available to the system, not a universal religious verdict.
